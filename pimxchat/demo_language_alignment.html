Language-Based Message Alignment Demo

Test how messages align based on the language direction

How it works:

RTL Languages (Right-to-Left): Persian, Arabic, Hebrew, etc.

LTR Languages (Left-to-Right): English, French, Spanish, etc.

When you type a message, the AI response will automatically align based on the detected language direction.

Try it out:

Example messages:

سلام، حال شما چطور است؟

سلام! ممنون، حال من خوب است. شما چطور هستید؟

Hello, how are you?

Hello! I'm doing well, thank you. How are you?
